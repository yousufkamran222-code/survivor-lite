# Decision log

Newest last. Each entry gives the decision, why it was made, and what would change it.

- **D-001 Genre: survivor-lite roguelite.** It had the best balance of buildable scope, ad creative and retention (see `HANDOFF.md`).
- **D-002 Engine: Godot 4 with the Compatibility renderer, pinned to 4.7.2** (latest stable on 2026-11-01). Plain-text project, runs headless in the cloud sandbox and in CI, and suits old GPUs and WebGL2.
- **D-003 Everything built from code.** One `.tscn` with one node; UI and rendering are created in scripts. Claude can't open the editor, and hand-edited `.tscn` files are fragile.
- **D-004 Simulation separate from rendering.** `World` is a pure `RefCounted` with packed arrays. This enables deterministic tests and a headless balance bot, and it is the cheapest per-enemy cost in GDScript. Rendering is immediate-mode shapes with no sprites, which gives a cohesive flat style with no art pipeline.
- **D-005 A home-made test runner instead of GUT or gdUnit4.** Zero dependencies, no addon to vendor, about 40 lines. Revisit if we need mocking or scene tests.
- **D-006 Working theme: microbes / petri dish (working title "Petri Panic").** It is open for discussion, and a re-theme is mostly a data edit (names, colours, text). The reasons for it:
  - Circles and blobs are the natural flat-shape art, so it looks intentional rather than placeholder.
  - It reads globally with no language or culture dependency, and it is family-safe for ads.
  - The ad creative is a dish filling with colourful germs.
  - The candidate twists to evaluate in M2 are "Mitosis" (enemies that split, already in) and "infection" (killed germs briefly fight for you).
- **D-007 The web build is a dev tool only.** The single-threaded web export (`variant/thread_support=false`) works on GitHub Pages without COOP/COEP headers. The product is the Android app.
- **D-008 Ads through an `Ads` autoload with swappable backends.** Web and tests use a stub that always rewards. AdMob comes in M3 behind the same API.
- **D-009 Balance targets are checked by a bot, then by humans.** The bot is a consistent stand-in and does not represent real players. Real playtest notes override it.
- **D-010 Fun pass inside the scope cap.** Yousuf chose all four: dash, elites + chests, evolutions and rare pickups. Evolutions live in their own table (max 4, one per weapon), so the ~10-upgrade pool stays small. Weapons target elites and the boss first, so the big rewards actually get earned.
- **D-011 Game modes instead of picking one design.** After playtesting, Yousuf liked both Classic and Territory, so each prototype becomes a selectable mode. A mode is pure data: balance overrides in `data/modes.json`, applied by `GameData.apply_mode()`. This keeps one codebase and one simulation, and lets each mode tune its own curve.
- **D-012 Spitter counter: Antibodies destroy enemy shots** (Yousuf's choice) instead of capping Spitters or scaling their damage. This turns the Antibodies build into a counter for ranged enemies.
- **D-013 Tower defence becomes a fourth mode, "Nucleus" (planned 2026-11-02).** Yousuf chose: enemies attack the nucleus but switch to the player when close; towers are built by standing on pads (one-thumb friendly); 12 numbered waves to win, then an optional endless Free Mode; only the nucleus dying ends the run (the player respawns). Towers reuse the weapon code. Full plan in `docs/ROADMAP.md` "Prototype 3".
- **D-014 Polish sweep (2026-11-02):** performance first, then combat feel, then the player cell, then the wider sweep (`docs/POLISH_PLAN.md`). Crits and streaks are visual only; the player stays a faceless cell; music is generated by the asset tool.
- **D-015 Music is CC0 tracks, not generated (2026-11-03).** Playtest feedback called the synthesised loops "trash"; Yousuf picked Juhani Junkala's "5 Chiptunes (Action)" (CC0, OpenGameArt) over the "4 Chiptunes (Adventure)" pack. Title Screen plays in menus, a random Level 1-3 per run, a 1.5 s cross-fade to a different level while a boss is alive, Ending on a win. The generator was removed from `tools/build_assets.py`.
- **D-016 Combat depth (2026-11-03):** Yousuf chose **Volatile** as a dash card (3 levels; offered only once the dash deals damage, via `requires_stat`) and a built-in **Outbreak meter** with **tap to trigger** (no free dashes during the frenzy: "too OP with all the dash buffs"). The UPGRADE button moved to a top-right pill (bottom-left clashed with the drag-anywhere joystick); the Outbreak button sits above DASH.
- **D-017 Outbreak = Plague Trail (2026-11-03).** The pickup Outbreak (bomb+vacuum+heal) worked as an extra life (Classic bot ~38% → ~83%) and was removed: Outbreak must be an *active* skill that creates an opportunity, like the dash. Yousuf chose Plague Trail (leave a toxic trail that infects germs; infections spread on death) over Mitosis; the boss challenge became a separate "Boss Rift" idea (SPEC "Later"). Constraint from Yousuf: Classic bot win rate must stay at or under 40-45%.
- **D-018 Outbreak = Frenzy v2 + per-mode difficulty (2026-11-03).** Plague Trail felt gimmicky (enemies die before it matters). Yousuf designed the frenzy: the original buffs plus half damage taken, x3 gold, boosted XP. Measuring showed Classic was already ~50% bot wins without Outbreak, so difficulty is now tuned per mode with `balance.difficulty` (enemy HP for Classic, damage taken for Swarm, since Swarm allies inherit enemy HP) to keep every mode at or under 40-45%.
- **D-019 Outbreak gold = gilding, not x3 (2026-11-03).** Yousuf's idea: on trigger, a no-damage 380 px gold blast coats nearby germs; each gilded germ pays `outbreak.gild_gold` (0.5, 10x a normal kill) when it dies (`World.e_gild`, `gilded_kills`). Visible, spatial and greedy, with no combat effect, so win rates are unchanged. x3 gold paid only a few gold per frenzy. `gold_mult` stays in code at 1.0.
- **D-020 Swarm orders + ally scaling (2026-11-03).** Allies dealt 2.5% of the damage, so the swarm didn't matter. Yousuf chose a fan-out order menu (Guard / Hunt / Harvest; Harvest fetches gems only), ally damage that scales with enemy HP and the player's might, and Outbreak boosting the swarm. Difficulty is held with player damage taken and the XP curve, not enemy HP (allies inherit it). The Sacrifice order is parked in SPEC "Later". What would change it: phone playtests saying damage taken x2 feels unfair, or one order always being right.
- **D-021 Late-game difficulty = bigger crowds + a final minute (2026-11-03).** Yousuf won two modes on his first try. He chose more satisfying pressure over HP sponges: more germs from 2:30 and a final-minute climax (faster germs, elites every 20 s, a spawn surge, a warning), plus slightly harder-hitting germs. Difficulty levels (unlock Lv 2+ per mode after a win) are agreed for later (SPEC "Later"). The revive stays as is (rewarded-ad value). The bot win rate becomes a sanity alarm (±10-15 points is fine); the target is a good player's first win in ~3-5 runs.
- **D-022 Fewer level-ups instead of more damage (2026-11-04).** Yousuf: damage modifiers feel right, but level-ups came every few seconds, which made runs too easy. A flat `xp.gain_mult` 0.7 in every mode (his "about 30%", not a big swing). If one mode feels slow, adjust its own XP curve in `modes.json`, not the global knob.
- **D-023 Kill streak lives in World, not the view (2026-11-04).** Yousuf wants future upgrades/abilities to trigger at streak counts, so the streak is simulation state (deterministic, game-time window, testable), with a data hook (`balance.streak.rewards`) that is empty for now. The window stays at 1.0 s and the tiers scale up to x500, because late-game killing is nearly continuous (a shorter window barely cut streaks). Best streak is a saved stat (Profile v3) with three achievements.
- **D-024 Level-up shockwave pushes, never damages (2026-11-04).** Yousuf: knock nearby germs back a bit, no damage. It's a feel beat (a breath of space on each level), so it can't become a weapon. Radius 220, knock 420 in `balance.xp.levelup_wave`.
