Petri Panic: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Petri Panic ("the game") is made by an independent developer ("we"). This page explains what data the game uses and why. The game has no accounts, no chat and no in-app purchases.

Data the game stores on your device

Your progress (gold, upgrades, unlocks, settings, records) is saved on your device only. On Android it may be included in your own Google device backup (Android Auto Backup), which you control in your device settings. We do not receive this data.

Advertising (Android app)

The Android version shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos (for example, to revive or double your gold) and occasional ads between runs. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID, device and app information (model, OS version, language)Showing ads, frequency capping, fraud prevention, measuring ad performance
Approximate location (from your IP address)Showing relevant ads and complying with local law
Interactions with adsMeasuring and paying for ads

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent first (Google's consent form, the User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options (ads). Where consent is refused, only non-personalised ads are shown. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Web versions

When you play in a browser, your progress is saved in that browser's storage. If you play on a game website (for example CrazyGames), that website's own privacy policy also applies to its pages, ads and accounts.

Crash reports and analytics

The game does not currently send analytics or crash reports. If this changes (for example, Google Firebase to find crashes and improve the game), this policy will be updated first and the change will be listed here.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

Because your progress stays on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. For data held by Google for advertising, use the ad consent options above or Google's own tools. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR) you may have further rights; contact us below and we will help.

Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page and update the date at the top.
